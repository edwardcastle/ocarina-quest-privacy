---
title: Ocarina Quest privacy policy
---


Last updated: 27 October 2026

Ocarina Quest collects no personal data. It has no accounts, no analytics
and no ads.

- **Microphone:** used on your phone only, to hear which note you play.
  In lessons and songs nothing is recorded. When you compose a song by
  playing it, the app keeps your playing so you can listen to it, and
  only if you choose “Keep it” when saving is it stored with the song,
  on your phone. Deleting the song deletes it. It is never sent anywhere
  by the app.
- **Song files and sharing:** a song file you import or open in the app
  is read on your phone and stays there. When you share one of your
  songs, the app hands the files you chose (a picture, the song file and,
  if you choose, your recording) to the app you pick, such as a chat or
  mail app, and that app’s privacy policy applies from there.
- **Progress:** your lessons, streak, settings and songs are stored on
  your phone, and the app sends them nowhere. If your phone backs apps up
  to your Google account, they travel with that backup under Google’s
  terms. Uninstalling the app removes them from the phone.
- **Purchases:** Ocarina Quest Pro is bought through Google Play, as a
  monthly or yearly subscription or once for life. Google Play processes
  the payment and manages subscriptions under
  [Google’s privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). When the
  app starts it asks Google Play whether this Google account owns Pro, and
  learns only that, not your payment details.
- **Updates:** the app asks Google Play whether a newer version exists;
  Google Play handles the update.
- **Ocarina link:** “Get a 12-hole alto C ocarina” opens Amazon’s website
  in your browser, where Amazon’s privacy policy applies.
- **Sheet music reading** (only in builds that include it; the Google Play
  version does not): the photo or PDF you pick is sent to the reading
  service, Anthropic, with the key you entered, under
  [Anthropic’s privacy policy](https://www.anthropic.com/privacy). Nothing
  is sent until you pick a file, and the app keeps no copy.
